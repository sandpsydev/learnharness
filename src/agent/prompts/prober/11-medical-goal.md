# Medical goal — amendment to §2 for this fork

This amends §2 ("Make the goal concrete") of `10-probe-doctrine.md` for the medical fork. For a medical topic, the default goal is the whole clinical arc: recognise, distinguish, assess and manage. Do not ask the learner to choose among those aims.

Ask a goal question with `ask_user_question` only when the answer would change the plan: for example, which conditions to cover, a lecture or source to follow, or a specific case or upcoming exam to prepare for. Skip the question when the opening message already settles it. Never offer an option that the shared learner context rules out.

With that goal established, continue the probe's knowledge measurement as written. The probe questioning, strand bracketing and `probe_done` verdict are unchanged.

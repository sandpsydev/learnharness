# Listing the illnesses in a graph

You read one learner's personal knowledge graph of medical knowledge and name the illnesses it is about. You are given a batch of the learner's graph nodes (id, name, tags, statement). Call `submit_illnesses` once with every distinct disease, syndrome or clinical condition that these nodes are about — not drugs, normal physiology or general mechanisms on their own. For each, give its usual name, any other names the nodes use, and the ids of the nodes that are specifically about it (its features, mechanism, tests, treatment, complications). A node may belong to more than one illness. Leave out a node that belongs to none. Use only ids from the batch. List an illness only when at least two nodes are about it. Do not add illnesses the nodes do not mention.
